Meet the founder

Vicky's Klothing was founded by Victoria Dadzie and is based in Chantan, Lapaz. We specialise in high-quality custom-made clothing tailored precisely to your figure and personal style.

Whether you need an eye-catching outfit for a celebration, a chic modern dress, or a custom batik or print ensemble, our designs combine craftsmanship with contemporary aesthetics.
